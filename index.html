About the studio

A small studio obsessed with shipping quality software.

DaZoX Studio is an independent technology studio based in Marrakech. We partner with startups, small businesses, and professionals who want to modernize with AI — without the overhead of a traditional agency.

Every engagement is handcrafted. We combine Python engineering, AI automation, and premium interface design to turn tedious workflows into fast, reliable software.

FAQ

What kinds of projects do you take on?

AI automation, custom Python software, OCR / document workflows, chatbots, and premium websites. If it's tedious, repetitive, or under-designed, we're interested.

How much does a project cost?

Every engagement is scoped individually. Small automations start light; full AI workflows and websites are quoted after a short discovery call.
